The member `enwik8` is the first 100,000,000 bytes of the English Wikipedia pages-articles dump dated 2006-03-03. Matt Mahoney published that prefix for the Large Text Compression Benchmark and the Hutter Prize. Wikipedia states that text published before 15 June 2009 was released under the GNU Free Documentation License. The current Wikipedia copyright page also allows reuse of article text under CC BY-SA 4.0, with the GFDL still applying where a page is not exclusively CC BY-SA.
